Services

We provide complete support from surveying and design through fabrication and installation, with a strong focus on accuracy, quality, and practical execution.

3D Laser Scanning

We provide high-precision 3D laser scanning using professional equipment capable of capturing accurate measurements across areas of up to 150 metres.

This technology allows us to generate detailed point cloud data, forming a reliable digital foundation for design, analysis, and fabrication. It significantly reduces measurement errors and ensures perfect alignment with existing site conditions.

Design & Engineering

We deliver fully tailored design solutions based on client requirements, technical constraints, and project objectives.

Our scope includes:

  • development of detailed 3D models
  • optimisation for manufacturability and installation
  • integration with existing structures and environments

Each design is created with a strong focus on precision, structural performance, and real-world feasibility, ensuring a seamless transition from concept to production.

Technical Documentation

We prepare comprehensive technical documentation required for fabrication and project execution.

Depending on project requirements, this may include:

  • detailed fabrication drawings
  • assembly documentation
  • technical specifications
  • engineering calculations where necessary

All documentation is developed to ensure clarity, accuracy, and full compliance with project standards.

Fabrication

We manufacture metal components and structures using advanced production technologies and proven fabrication methods.

Our capabilities include:

  • welding (steel, stainless steel, aluminium, brass)
  • laser cutting
  • bending and forming
  • CNC machining and precision processing

We ensure that every project is delivered using the most suitable production methods — whether in-house or through trusted specialist partners — maintaining full control over quality and consistency throughout.

Installation

We provide professional on-site installation, ensuring all components are assembled and fitted according to design specifications.

Our team ensures precise alignment, structural integrity, and high-quality finishing, delivering results that meet both technical and visual expectations.
