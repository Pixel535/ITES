About Us

We are a UK-based company specialising in the design and manufacturing of metal components and structural solutions. With over 15 years of experience, we support our clients in delivering projects of all sizes, from concept to completion.

At the core of our work is a strong commitment to precision, quality, and attention to detail at every stage of the process. We believe that even the smallest elements can make the biggest difference in the final outcome.

What We Do

We provide comprehensive support across the entire lifecycle of a project. Whether you need a single component or a complex structural solution, we are equipped to deliver with accuracy and efficiency.

We operate across several key sectors:

  • Airport engineering – delivering components and structures for airport environments
  • Architectural details – custom-made elements for interiors and exteriors
  • Architectural metalwork – structural features for buildings and public spaces
  • Structural steelwork – infrastructure and construction solutions
  • Movie props – bespoke fabrication for film and media

Certificates

We work in accordance with recognized industry standards and hold certifications including UKCA and ISO 3834-3, reflecting our commitment to quality, compliance, and controlled manufacturing processes. We are also working towards Achilles Building Confidence Gold (Desktop) accreditation.

UKCA Compliant ISO 3834-3 Certified

All of our site staff hold relevant CSCS cards and SMSTS/SSSTS qualifications with supporting training records and have vast cumulative experience of 40 years. ITES also provides bespoke lifting services. We are experts of moving heavy structures and machinery with experience of lifting and moving X-ray machines in airport environment, turbines in power plants and complex structure building installations, etc... We develop the lift plans and provide lifting AP services, lifting supervision as well as slinging and backing the lifts. Copies of our Training Matrix are available on request.

Our services include:

  • Design and development of metal structures
  • Manufacturing of custom components
  • Bespoke architectural and decorative metalwork, including custom-designed visual elements such as signage, feature installations, and aesthetic solutions tailored to enhance spaces without compromising functionality
  • Technical support and project coordination
